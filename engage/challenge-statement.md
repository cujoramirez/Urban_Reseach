## Big Idea & Essential Question (CBL framing)

- **Big Idea:** *Urban Living Experience.*
- **Essential Question:** *"Apa yang menentukan apakah berjalan kaki di kota terasa nyaman,
  aman, dan bermartabat, serta bagi siapa pengalaman itu benar-benar terwujud?"*
  (What determines whether walking in the city feels comfortable, safe, and dignified, and for
  whom is that experience actually realized?)

## Refined statement (2026-06-19, post-fieldwork — proposed)

> **EN:** *"Make walking the Sudirman corridor comfortable **and safe** — through the heat of
> the day, into the night, and beyond the main trotoar to the streets one block behind it."*
>
> **ID:** *"Membuat berjalan kaki di koridor Sudirman nyaman **dan aman** — di tengah panas
> siang, hingga malam hari, dan tidak hanya di trotoar utama tetapi juga di jalan-jalan satu
> blok di belakangnya."*

### How Field Day 1 reshaped the wording (evidence in [`../investigate/synthesis.md`](../investigate/synthesis.md))

- **"More comfortable" is no longer about building a better sidewalk.** The main trotoar is
  widely judged *already good* ("lega, manusiawi, bagus") — the old framing that people avoid a
  good sidewalk was **not supported** on the main corridor. So the problem sits *on top of* a
  good sidewalk, not in it.
- **The strongest comfort barrier we found is thermal — but we hold it as the strongest signal,
  not the only one.** Heat was the most common complaint, on a measured **33–35 °C feels-like**,
  UV 7–8 day (`weather_2026-06-18.csv`).
- **"Safe" is added because the data demanded it, and it is time-and-place specific:** safe by
  day (incl. a woman walking home at 01:00) but risky at **lit-but-isolated** spots after ~22:00
  (JPO, parking). Plus micro-hazards (steep/slippery kerb-to-crossing ramp; ojol on the trotoar).
- **The gradient — the locked core of our scope — is still the open question.** It was barely
  sampled on Day 1; Saturday's round tests it. The refined statement keeps it in view rather
  than dropping it.
- **"For whom" matters:** workers, commuters, elderly, students, and women report differently.

### Scope reminder (this part *is* locked)

The Sudirman corridor **and its immediate back-streets**, referenced by **named streets
only**. The heart of the study is the **contrast** between the wide, proper Sudirman trotoar
and the near-nonexistent sidewalks one block behind it. See [`../DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md).

### What we are deliberately NOT doing yet

- Not naming a solution (no app, route, sensor, or feature). The challenge is a *problem*.
- Not treating heat as the sole problem, nor the gradient as proven — Saturday's data may shift
  the weighting. Keep the statement open.

## Note on the word "comfortable"

We are using "comfortable" provisionally. We have **not** yet established that discomfort is
the actual problem, or what comfort means to pedestrians here. One of our guiding questions
asks exactly that (see [`guiding-questions.md`](guiding-questions.md)). Keep the statement
open to being reframed by what we find.

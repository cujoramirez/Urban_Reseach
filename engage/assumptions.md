# Our Assumptions (UNVERIFIED)

Everything on this page is something we currently **believe** about walking in the Sudirman
area but have **not yet checked** against reality. These are *hypotheses to test in the
field*, not facts.

> ⚠️ **Keep assumptions OUT of the guiding questions.** An assumption written into a question
> turns it into a leading question and biases the whole study. Assumptions live *here*. The
> questions in [`guiding-questions.md`](guiding-questions.md) stay neutral.

## How to use this file

- Populate it from our Miro board, pull out every belief we are carrying into the field.
- Label each one **UNVERIFIED** until fieldwork confirms or refutes it.
- After fieldwork, update the **Status** column. Do **not** delete a refuted assumption,
  a refuted assumption is a finding.

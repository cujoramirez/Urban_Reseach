# Fieldworker Self-Presentation — how to blend in (and stay safe)

How **we** should look and behave so our presence doesn't distort what we observe or scare off
who we want to talk to. Blending in here means **reducing the observer effect** and showing
respect for the setting — not deceiving anyone. We still introduce ourselves and get consent for
every interview (see [`instruments/intercept-script.md`](instruments/intercept-script.md)).

> Why this matters: if we read as outsiders, surveyors, or enforcement, people change their
> behaviour and their answers. Vendors and ojol drivers in particular will scatter from anyone
> who looks like Satpol PP / enforcement. Looking like we belong gets honest, ordinary behaviour.

---

## Match the setting (the corridor has two very different "dress codes")

### On the main Sudirman/Thamrin trotoar — look like a young professional
- The weekday crowd is **office workers / private employees** \[S1, S9]. Aim for **smart casual /
  business casual**: collared/neat shirt or blouse, plain trousers/skirt, closed shoes, simple
  bag. An academy/student lanyard is fine — it reads "intern", which fits.
- **Avoid:** anything that reads **enforcement/authority** (tactical/ID-vest, all-black "petugas"
  look) — it biases vendors/ojol; and anything that reads **tourist** (big camera, shorts +
  daypack) — it draws attention and touts.

### On the back-streets / kampung (Karet, Setiabudi, Menteng Atas) — dress down, neat and modest
- These are **dense residential kampung** \[round-3 leads]. Smart office wear will stand out here
  as much as scruffy wear stands out on the main trotoar. Go **casual, modest, unflashy**: plain
  t-shirt/shirt, modest trousers, simple shoes. Leave valuables and flashy gear behind.
- **Greet local gatekeepers** (RT/RW, warung owner, vendor association) before observing/asking.
  A friendly "permisi" and a short explanation buys trust.

### Weekend / Car Free Day
- Crowd is **leisure** (joggers, cyclists, families). Casual sportswear blends fine.

---

## Practical kit (Jakarta heat + long audits)
The corridor is exposed with **limited shade** \[S1] — dress and pack for it:
- Light, breathable clothing; **water**; hat or **umbrella** (sun and sudden rain); comfortable
  closed shoes for hours of walking/auditing; small rain layer.
- **Discreet tools:** count and note on a **phone**, not a big clipboard (a clipboard reads
  "surveyor/authority" and changes behaviour). For photos, follow
  [`field/photos/README.md`](photos/README.md): document the *environment*, **blur faces**, no
  intrusive close-ups.

---

## Safety (non-negotiable)
- **Female-led** for sensitive and **after-dark** intercepts; never solo for those \[intercept-
  script].
- **Buddy system** everywhere off the main trotoar; share live location with the team.
- Do the **back-streets in daylight first**; only revisit after dark once you know the area and
  with a local contact.
- Trust your read of a situation — **disengage** politely if anything feels off, for you or the
  respondent.
- Keep the academy student ID; be ready to plainly say who you are and why you're there if asked.

---

## Profile ourselves honestly
- We're **Apple Developer Academy students** — on the main corridor the young-professional look is
  basically who we are, so it's authentic, not a costume.
- **Check each team member:** anyone whose usual style would stand out in a given setting should
  adjust for that shift. Match clothing to the **sub-area and time** you're assigned that day.
- Split roles so the team isn't a conspicuous cluster of 5: pairs for back-streets, spread out
  for counts on the main trotoar.

---

## Ethics line (so "blending in" stays clean)
- Blending = **lower our footprint and respect the place**. It is **not** pretending to be
  someone with authority, and **not** secret recording.
- **Observation** is of public, aggregate behaviour (counts, no identifying detail).
- **Interviews** always start with the consent line; recording needs separate explicit consent
  \[intercept-script]. If anyone asks us to stop, we stop.

# Respondent Profiles & Raw Notulen — Field Day 1 (2026-06-18)

> ⚠️ **READ THIS FIRST — status of the numbers.**
> The 1–10 scales in Part A below are an **interpretation derived from the audio
> transcripts**, not values the respondent was asked to give (most intercepts did not
> pose a 1–10 question). Treat them as the analyst's coding, **not measured data**, and
> always defer to the verbatim transcripts (`2026-06-18-intercept-transcripts.md`).
> The Tim A / Tim B field notulen in Part B *do* contain some explicitly-stated scales
> (where a respondent actually answered "7/10", etc.) — those are marked there.
>
> **Scale direction for "Suhu (cuaca)":** 1 = sangat teduh/dingin, 10 = sangat panas/terik.
> (So a *high* heat score = more discomfort from heat.)

- **Sample:** 16 people total across two teams — **10 upper/middle class, 6 bottom class**
  (team's own classification). 12 audio-recorded intercepts + additional notulen-only contacts.
- **Coverage caveat:** nearly all contacts were on the **main Sudirman trotoar / station /
  JPO / tunnel** — the *back-streets one block away* (the "gradient", Q4) were **not** sampled.

## Part A — Interpreted profiles from the 12 audio files

### 1. Bapak Kritikus Tata Kota — File 11
- 🕚 Fleksibel · 📍 Trotoar Sudirman
- **Insight:** Critiques post-revitalisation city design as misguided — comparative study
  trips ("studi banding") went to four-season countries for a tropical climate.
- ‼️ **Pain point:** Midday very hot (trees not yet effective); at night it is dark because
  foliage covers the lamp poles. Too many mosquitoes to relax in the evening.
- 🎯 **Goal:** Facilities suited to a tropical climate and local behaviour, not copied European bike lanes.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 7/10 · kenyamanan 4/10 (frustrated with design) · suhu 10/10.

### 2. Pegawai MITJ — File 08
- 🕚 Pagi (~05.18) · 📍 Stasiun Sudirman
- **Insight:** Knows the street history well (former car U-turn that was closed, used to be
  begal-prone). Appreciates the TOD integration.
- ‼️ **Pain point:** Ongoing construction (continued revitalisation / statue roundabout "donat")
  can partly block the way.
- 🎯 **Goal:** Road-widening and the Sudirman statue relocation go smoothly to untangle the area.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 8/10 (was begal-prone, now safe) · kenyamanan 8/10 · suhu 5/10.

### 3. Bapak Jalan (Lansia) — File 04
- 🕚 Pagi/Siang · 📍 Rute Thamrin City – Semanggi – HI
- **Insight:** Walks ~1.5 h daily for exercise. Very satisfied with the revitalised trotoar.
- ‼️ **Pain point:** Specific spots (near the Japanese Embassy / Sarinah) still feel fairly hot.
- 🎯 **Goal:** Keep the trotoar — it strongly supports light physical activity for the elderly.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 9/10 (reassured by police presence) · kenyamanan 9/10 · suhu 6/10.

### 4. Mba Tangerang Selatan — File 05
- 🕚 Pagi/Siang · 📍 Halte TJ CSW – Setiabudi
- **Insight:** Helped by the presence of other pedestrians (not alone). Always carries an umbrella.
- ‼️ **Pain point:** The trotoar→zebra-cross drop is too high/steep and slippery (her friend fell —
  and fell again during the interview). Motorbikes also run the crossing.
- 🎯 **Goal:** Make the ramp to the road gentler and safer.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 7/10 (fall + motorbike risk) · kenyamanan 6/10 · suhu 8/10.

### 5. Pria Pejalan Kaki Mingguan — File 03
- 🕚 Siang (counter-commute) · 📍 Halte Dukuh Atas – Stasiun Sudirman
- **Insight:** Walks once a week when it's quiet; feels *relatively safer* precisely when it's
  not crowded.
- ‼️ **Pain point:** Direct sun, feels hot.
- 🎯 **Goal:** Add canopies as shade.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 8/10 · kenyamanan 6/10 · suhu 8/10.

### 6. Petugas Keamanan Kawasan — File 01
- 🕚 Fleksibel / shift · 📍 Sudirman (MRT/KAI area)
- **Insight:** Each operator guards its own assets; there's a coverage gap *outside* the gates
  (public space).
- ‼️ **Pain point:** No good security integration between transport operators (KAI, MRT) and local police.
- 🎯 **Goal:** Stronger protection and cooperation, not buck-passing between zones/agencies.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 6/10 (overwhelmed re: authorities) · kenyamanan 7/10 · suhu 5/10.

### 7. Bapak Bawa Anak — File 10
- 🕚 Siang · 📍 Arah Universitas – Stasiun
- **Insight:** Likes the buffer between trotoar and main road — pollution-free, leafy, good for a child.
- ‼️ **Pain point:** The drop to the zebra cross is too steep; worried about fast vehicles from the main road.
- 🎯 **Goal:** Make crossings friendlier for people with children/families.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 8/10 · kenyamanan 7/10 · suhu 7/10.

### 8. Pria Pejalan Kaki Harian — File 02
- 🕚 Siang · 📍 Jalan Raya Sudirman
- **Insight:** Enjoys walking the corridor because the trees keep it cool even when it's hot.
- ‼️ **Pain point:** Midday heat.
- 🎯 **Goal:** Keep the shaded, leafy environment.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 9/10 · kenyamanan 8/10 · suhu 7/10.

### 9. Anak Sekolah SMAN 3 (Teladan) — File 06
- 🕚 School hours · 📍 Rute Palmerah – SMAN 3
- **Insight:** Daily commuters by TransJakarta; no fear walking together with friends.
- ‼️ **Pain point:** None significant (no catcalling / nothing suspicious experienced).
- 🎯 **Goal:** Keep the corridor safe and comfortable for students.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 9/10 · kenyamanan 9/10 · suhu 5/10.

### 10. Pengendara Motor dari Tebet — File 07
- 🕚 Fleksibel · 📍 Parkir koridor Sudirman
- **Insight:** Mostly travels by private motorbike; only walks short distances around parking.
- ‼️ **Pain point:** Midday temperature too extreme to enjoy on foot.
- 🎯 **Goal:** Extra shade by day (night is already enjoyable).
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 8/10 · kenyamanan 7/10 · suhu 8/10.

### 11. Informan Keamanan Titik Rawan — File 09
- 🕚 Malam (≥22.00) · 📍 Area parkir & JPO atas
- **Insight:** Crime (copet, curanmor, jambret) has fallen over the last ~6 months but the
  potential remains if people aren't alert. Hotspots: parking area (below) and the JPO (above).
- ‼️ **Pain point:** The upper JPO is too deserted late at night — *lit but isolated* — a target spot.
- 🎯 **Goal:** People shouldn't walk alone after 22.00 in visually-bright-but-isolated areas.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 5/10 (night specifically) · kenyamanan 7/10 · suhu 5/10.

### 12. Satpam / Satpol PP (mobile) — File 12
- 🕚 Shift (mobile) · 📍 Pos Polda Bawah – Summitmas – Patung Pemuda
- **Insight:** Trotoar is wide and very adequate, but PKL (Starling/siomay) create a dilemma —
  liked by some pedestrians yet against the rules.
- ‼️ **Pain point:** Ojek-online riders forcing their way onto the trotoar; little mass shelter
  when it suddenly rains. (Note: motorbike enforcement is Dishub's remit, not Satpol PP's.)
- 🎯 **Goal:** Motorbike compliance so they stop taking the pedestrian lane as a shortcut.
- Scales *(interpreted)*: keamanan 6/10 (motorbikes on trotoar) · kenyamanan 8/10 · suhu 8/10.

# S26: 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design (U.S. DOJ / Access Board)

## Full citation
U.S. Department of Justice. (2010). *2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design*. Washington, DC.
(Technical provisions derive from the U.S. Access Board's ADA Accessibility Guidelines.)

- **Source:** https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/design-standards/2010-stds/
- **Full PDF:** https://archive.ada.gov/regs2010/2010ADAStandards/2010ADAStandards_prt.pdf
- **Ramp guidance:** https://www.access-board.gov/ada/guides/chapter-4-ramps-and-curb-ramps/
- **Source type:** National standard (US) — the de-facto international dimensional reference.
- **Accessed:** 2026-06-28

## Scope
**STANDARD (international reference, dimensions).** Freely published and exhaustively dimensioned —
the most-cited concrete accessibility benchmark worldwide. Use for the numbers ISO 21542 keeps behind
a paywall and where Permen PUPR is silent.

## QUANTITATIVE standards (exact passages: audit benchmarks)
- **Ramp running slope ≤ 1:12** (§405.2): "Ramp runs shall have a running slope not steeper than 1:12."
- **Maximum rise per run = 30 in (760 mm)** (§405.6): "The rise for any ramp run shall be 30 inches
  (760 mm) maximum."
- **Ramp cross slope ≤ 1:48** (§405.3).
- **Ramp clear width ≥ 36 in (915 mm)** (§405.5).
- **Landing length ≥ 60 in (1525 mm)** (§405.7).
- **Door clear width ≥ 32 in (815 mm)**, measured with the door open 90° (§404.2.3).
- Wheelchair turning space / clear floor ≈ **60 in (1525 mm)** where required.

## Why it matters
- Supplies the precise on-site audit thresholds (ramp 1:12, door 815 mm, landing 1525 mm).
- Globally recognized citation; pairs with Permen PUPR (S29) for the local-vs-international comparison
  the baseline table makes.

## Flags
- US legal scope; we use it as a **technical** benchmark, not a legal requirement in Indonesia. Where
  it and Permen PUPR differ (e.g. ramp 1:12 vs PUPR ~1:10 indoor), the baseline scores against the
  **stricter** value for safety.

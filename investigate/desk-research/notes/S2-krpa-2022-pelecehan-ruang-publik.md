## Scope
**CONTEXT — NATIONAL.** 4,236 respondents across **34 provinces**. **No Jakarta-only or
Sudirman-specific breakdown** appears in this deck. Use only as background on harassment in
public space; do **not** present as corridor evidence.

## QUANTITATIVE findings (exact figures; slides 4–6, cross-checked against rendered images)
- **"Survei … diikuti 4.236 responden dari 34 provinsi di seluruh Indonesia"** (slide 4).
- **"3.037 responden mengatakan mengalami pelecehan"** (slide 5).
- By group (slide 5):
  - Women: **78,89%** ("4 dari 5 perempuan di Indonesia mengalami pelecehan seksual di ruang
    publik").
  - Men: **29,6%** ("3 dari 10 laki-laki").
  - Other genders: **83,33%** ("4 dari 5 teman-teman gender lainnya").
- **Locations — offline public space, highest (respondents could pick >1)** (slide 6,
  "Lokasi Terjadinya Pelecehan, ruang publik luring/offline"):
  - **jalanan umum (public streets): 2.130**
  - kawasan pemukiman (residential areas): 797
  - transportasi umum (public transport): 693
  - toko/mall/pusat perbelanjaan: 432
  - tempat kerja (workplace): 377
- Online harassment top channels (slide 7): media sosial 1.248, aplikasi chat 998, etc.
  (context only).
- **Forms of offline harassment, highest** (slide 10, multi-select): **siulan/catcalling
  2.026**, komentar atas tubuh 934, main mata 893, komentar seksis/seksual 795, diklakson 724,
  disentuh 602.
- **Perpetrator identity, highest** (slide 12, multi-select): **orang tak dikenal/stranger
  2.454**, teman 669, kolega/teman kerja 332, penyedia transportasi 295, tetangga 223, anggota
  keluarga 216, guru/dosen 134, tenaga kesehatan 44.
- **Impact** (slide 13): **70,56%** of the 3.037 who were harassed said it **worsened their
  situation/feelings** during the pandemic (discomfort, irritation, anger recurring).

## SENTIMENT / INTENT
- Frames public streets as the **single most common site** of offline harassment in Indonesia —
  relevant to the "comfort/safety of walking" question, especially for women.
- Conclusion (slide 14): "**perempuan dan kelompok gender minoritas lainnya 6 kali lebih rentan**
  untuk mengalami pelecehan seksual di ruang publik"; public space "masih belum cukup aman."
- KRPA's stated aim: "menghentikan kekerasan di ruang publik, dan menciptakan ruang publik yang
  aman bagi perempuan dan kelompok marjinal lainnya" (slide 2).

## QUALITATIVE
- Survey design and recommendations target central/regional government and service units to
  ensure safe access to public space (slide 3). No interview narratives in this deck.

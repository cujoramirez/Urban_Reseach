# S4: Revitalisasi Trotoar Bangkitkan Budaya Berjalan Kaki (Dinas Bina Marga DKI)

## Full citation
Berita Jakarta. (2019, 18 October). *Revitalisasi Trotoar Bangkitkan Budaya Berjalan Kaki.*
Berita Jakarta (official Pemprov DKI Jakarta news outlet). Figures attributed to Hari Nugroho,
Kepala Dinas Bina Marga DKI Jakarta.

- **URL:** https://m.beritajakarta.id/read/73289/revitalisasi-trotoar-bangkitkan-budaya-berjalan-kaki
- **Capture:** `sources/web-captures.md` → CAP-1
- **Source type:** Government / news (official outlet).
- **Accessed:** 2026-06-16

## Scope
**CORRIDOR + CONTEXT.** Sudirman–Thamrin sidewalk width is corridor-specific; the program
totals (km, budget) are city-wide context.

## QUANTITATIVE findings (exact passages)
- **"sejak 2017 hingga 2019, revitalisasi trotoar di Ibukota tercatat telah dilakukan sepanjang
  134 kilometer."**
- Sudirman–MH Thamrin trotoar built **"antara 8-12 meter"** wide; completed **2018**;
  accessible for persons with disabilities, children, elderly; includes bike lanes, green space,
  cultural-expression areas, "walk of fame."
- 2019 program: **51 locations** across the five Jakarta municipalities; budget **Rp 327
  billion**. 2020 target: **47 kilometers**.

## POLICY
- Demonstrates an explicit **2017–2022 sidewalk-revitalization policy** led by Dinas Bina Marga,
  with the wide Sudirman–Thamrin trotoar as its flagship. Useful as the policy anchor for the
  expert-interview "obstacles" angle and for dating the corridor's current form.

## QUALITATIVE / SENTIMENT
- Official framing: revitalization intended to "bangkitkan budaya berjalan kaki" (revive a
  walking culture). This is the *government’s stated intent*, keep labelled as such, not as an
  outcome measure.

## Corridor vs. general
- **Corridor:** the 8–12 m Sudirman–Thamrin width and 2018 completion.
- **Context:** 134 km / 51 sites / Rp 327 bn / 47 km target are DKI-wide.

## Flags
- Single-source, official outlet → figures are the agency's own; corroborate the program scale
  against S6 (ITDP) and any Bina Marga open data where possible.
- The width "8–12 m" is the **main corridor**; it says nothing about the back-streets (G1).

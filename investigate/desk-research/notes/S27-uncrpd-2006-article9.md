# S27: UN CRPD — Article 9 (Accessibility), 2006

## Full citation
United Nations. (2006). *Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities*, Article 9 —
Accessibility. New York: UN. **Indonesia ratified via UU No. 19/2011.**

- **Source:** https://www.un.org/development/desa/disabilities/convention-on-the-rights-of-persons-with-disabilities/article-9-accessibility.html
- **Source type:** International human-rights treaty (binding on Indonesia).
- **Accessed:** 2026-06-28

## Scope
**FRAMEWORK (rights).** The *why* of the project: accessibility as a right enabling independent
living and full participation — exactly the "independence" value Persona 4 and Ci Jessi stressed.

## Verified passages
- **Art. 9(1) (exact):** "To enable persons with disabilities to live independently and participate
  fully in all aspects of life, States Parties shall take appropriate measures to ensure to persons
  with disabilities access, on an equal basis with others, to the physical environment, to
  transportation, to information and communications…"
- **Art. 9(2)(a):** measures apply to "Buildings, roads, transportation and other indoor and outdoor
  facilities, including schools, housing, medical facilities and workplaces."
- **Art. 9(2)(e):** "live assistance and intermediaries, including guides…" — assistance is a
  *complement* to accessible design, not a substitute (supports the audit's independence/dignity test).

## Why it matters
- Grounds the framing in a binding rights instrument (Indonesia bound via UU 19/2011 + UU 8/2016):
  "live independently" is the product's north star, not a nice-to-have.
- Justifies the dignity/independence criterion — being carried by staff is **not** accessibility.

## Flags
- Principle-level, not dimensional. Pair with S26 / S29 for measurable criteria.

## Scope
**CORRIDOR-SPECIFIC — but only Segment 4** of the Sudirman corridor (southern end). The paper
divides the corridor into four segments north→south by land use; it studies **segment 4**,
whose crowd points are **in front of Menara Mandiri, GBK & FX, and Ratu Plaza** (near
Transjakarta/MRT transit integrated with JPO). Studies the **post-revitalization (2018–2020)**
state. This is exactly the southern part of our scope (toward Senayan).

## Method (for weighting the evidence)
- PEQI (Pedestrian Environmental Quality Index), adapted to Indonesia.
- Two tracks: (a) **physical condition** by field observation + scoring (ordinal 1–5);
  (b) **pedestrian perception** by questionnaire. Final quality = average of the two.
- Sample target 96 (Lemeshow, 90% accuracy); **105 respondents** used (Tabel 9).
- Non-probability accidental sampling.

## QUANTITATIVE findings (exact passages)
- **Physical-condition PEQI = 64.28.** "𝑆𝑘𝑜𝑟 𝑃𝐸𝑄𝐼 = (125 − 35) x 100 / (175 − 35) = 9000/140 =
  64,28" → Class II, "sesuai dengan sebagian besar standar desain."
  - Category scores: **kelengkapan 61.4**, **keselamatan 67.6**, **kenyamanan 60.7**.
  - Worst components (score 1/5 = "tidak sesuai"): **muka bangunan aktif**, **blok bangunan**,
    **lampu pejalan kaki** (pedestrian signals), **tempat sampah** (bins).
  - Weak (2/5 "kurang sesuai"): **fasilitas peneduh** (shade), slope & side-flares of the JPO.
- **Perception PEQI = 84.70.** "(7372 − 1680) x 100 / (8400 − 1680) = 569200/6720 = 84,70" →
  Class I, "optimal."
  - Sub-scores (sum over 105 resp.): keamanan category **89.2**, kenyamanan category **82.7**.
  - Lowest perceived: **konektivitas terhadap titik transit 383**, **kebisingan 370**,
    **fasilitas peneduh 389** (all "cukup/ sesuai" but lowest of the set).
- **Combined PEQI = 74.49.** "(64,28 + 84,70)/2 = 148,98/2 = 74,49" → Class II, **"dapat
  diterima pejalan kaki"** (acceptable).

## QUALITATIVE findings (exact passages / close paraphrase)
- Persistent problem (intro + findings): **"penyalahgunaan ruang sirkulasi pejalan kaki
  sebagai ruang melintas dan parkir sepeda motor, dan sebagai lapak berjualan PKL, karena
  penempatan dan penyusunan bollard yang belum mencegah sepeda motor masuk jalur pejalan
  kaki."** → bollards fail to keep motorbikes and street vendors (PKL) off the sidewalk; this
  "mengurangi ruang gerak, mengancam keselamatan, dan mengurangi kenyamanan."
- **Shade:** "Jalur pejalan kaki segmen keempat tidak memiliki cukup banyak fasilitas peneduh"
  → discomfort in bad weather; trees in the green strip don't shade or damp noise.
- **Crossings:** "Tidak tersedia lampu pejalan kaki pada seluruh persimpangan" and missing
  crossing medians at some intersections.
- **Transit friction:** rebuild of Halte Transjakarta GBK reduced connectivity; "Banyak pejalan
  kaki merasa jarak terhadap titik transit yang cukup jauh atau lama."
- Other: broken/unmaintained bins; benches lack backrests/armrests/shade; frequent construction
  disrupts flow at peak.

## SENTIMENT / INTENT (exact passages)
- **"pejalan kaki lebih peduli terhadap pemenuhan kebutuhan kenyamanannya daripada
  keamanannya"** — pedestrians care more about *comfort* than *safety* here, because most
  problems are comfort-related.
- **"karakteristik pejalan kaki cenderung menerima kualitas jalur pejalan kaki selama mampu
  mewadahi pergerakannya"** — pedestrians tend to *accept/tolerate* the sidewalk as long as it
  lets them move, even where it misses design standards. (Explains why perception (84.7) scores
  higher than measured physical condition (64.3).)

## User characteristics (context for our fieldwork)
- Dominated by **male office workers, age 19–40, normal mobility**, using the sidewalk
  routinely Mon–Fri to commute via inter-modal transit; mostly travelling **alone**.
- Peaks: **06:00–09:00** and **15:00–18:00 WIB**.

## Corridor vs. general
- **Corridor-specific:** all scores, problems, user profile above (Sudirman segment 4).
- **General-Jakarta note (their words):** "Mayoritas jalur pejalan kaki pada kawasan perkotaan
  di Indonesia, termasuk … Sudirman … belum dapat menyelesaikan permasalahan seperti peneduhan,
  kebisingan, dan penyalahgunaan ruang."

## Flags
- **VERIFY:** physical vs. perception gap (64.3 vs 84.7) is acknowledged by the authors as
  partly a **method artifact** (perception questionnaire less detailed, questions hard to
  understand → subjective answers "kurang akurat"). Cite the gap carefully, not as proof of
  comfort.
- Segment 4 only → do **not** generalize these scores to the whole corridor (esp. the northern
  Dukuh Atas/Bundaran HI end).

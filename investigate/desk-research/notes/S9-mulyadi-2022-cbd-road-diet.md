## Scope
**CORRIDOR-SPECIFIC** — Sudirman–Thamrin CBD. Five survey locations along the corridor (3 on
Sudirman incl. Skate Park area; 2 on Thamrin incl. Plaza area), plus the HI roundabout area.
Pilot project of the **road-diet** sidewalk revitalization for the 2018 Asian Games. This is the
strongest corridor-specific *before/after* walkability source in the set.

## Method
- **Modified Global Walkability Index (GWI)**, 9 parameters, 5-point rating (1–5); plus
  **Importance–Performance Analysis (IPA)**; Pearson correlation. Pedestrian-perception based.
- GWI category scale (their Table, src Lin et al. 2009):
  **WI ≥ 70 = "highly walkable" (green); 50 < WI < 70 = "waiting to walk" (amber);
  WI ≤ 50 = "not walkable" (red).**

## QUANTITATIVE findings (exact passages)
- **Headline:** "revitalization of sidewalk using the road diet concept can increase walkability
  up to **38.98%**." (abstract + conclusion)
- **Public transport:** "increasing the number of public transportation users by **15.41%**."
  Mechanism: TransJakarta BRT **Corridor 1** (through Sudirman) passengers **2017 = 24,870,678
  /year → 2018 = 28,703,262 /year** = +15.41% (DKI Jakarta Government, 2020).
- **Post-revitalization walkability (this study):** five locations all **"highly walkable,"**
  WI totals **88.60, 88.95, 85.6, 84.35, 86.8** (range 84.35–88.95); HI-roundabout area 88.60 &
  88.95.
- **2015 baseline (citing Ridhani & Christanto 2015 = our S5):** WI "relatively lower **about 64
  and 71 at Sudirman road and Thamrin Road, respectively**," i.e. **Sudirman = "waiting to walk,"
  Thamrin = "walkable."** (See CONTRADICTION below.)
- **Respondent profile:** majority **private employees (44%)**; high income >IDR 7,000,000
  (>USD 500) 44%; ages 26–35 and 36–46; education undergrad/HS/diploma 30.5%.
- Context: Jakarta TransJakarta/integration passengers "more than 156% in 2019 … 264,032,780";
  Jakarta won Sustainable Transport Award (STA); 2021 ranked 46th most congested city (down from
  2017).

## QUALITATIVE / IPA findings
- Main walkability obstacles named: **obstructions on the sidewalk** and **sidewalk width**.
- IPA flags items to "concentrate here" (high importance, low performance) — incl. pedestrian
  conflicts, motorist behavior, and infrastructure for people with disabilities (P-items).

## SENTIMENT / INTENT
- Frames the road diet as a **success** that makes Jakarta "more walkable" while raising PT use;
  recommends periodic evaluation and replication in other CBDs. (Author stance — label as such.)

## Corridor vs. general

- City-wide items (STA award, congestion rank, total TJ passengers) = context.

## Scope
**CORRIDOR-ADJACENT.** Dukuh Atas is the **northern transit hub of the Sudirman corridor**
(MRT/KRL/Transjakarta/LRT interchange near the Bundaran HI end) — described as the "embrio
pembangunan TOD di Indonesia" with a **walkable-city / road-diet** concept. Data collected
**2019** (post-revitalization era, pre/early-MRT). Complements S1 (which covers the southern
segment 4) by covering the northern node.

## Method
- Quantitative; **300 respondents** (random) in the Dukuh Atas TOD area; environmental data via
  secondary + spatial (satellite) data; descriptive statistics + partial-T linear regression.

## QUANTITATIVE findings (exact / close passages)
- **Headline result (abstract):** "pendapatan, maksud perjalanan, dan kepadatan permukiman
  dapat mempengaruhi frekuensi pejalan kaki di kawasan TOD Dukuh Atas" — **income, trip purpose,
  and settlement density** significantly affect walking frequency (significance <0.05).
- Pedestrian profile: **±63% male**; majority **age 15–30 (usia produktif)**; **~89% private
  employees (karyawan swasta)**; morning peak **06.00–09.00 WIB**.
- Access: a large share reach the transit centre in **<10 minutes / ~5 minutes' walk**;
  non-residential land use share ~58% in the area. *(Two-column PDF; see VERIFY.)*
- Final regression model reported as: **Y = 2,022 + 0,074(X1.5) + 0,108(X1.7)** with component
  significances (e.g., 0,040 and 0,002, <0,05).

## QUALITATIVE / SENTIMENT
- Supports walkability as integral to TOD success at Dukuh Atas; walking chosen partly because
  it is **costless** and for short transit access; people will walk when destinations are within
  a short walk of transit.

## Corridor vs. general
- **Corridor-adjacent (Dukuh Atas node).** International comparisons (Iran, US Treasure Coast)
  are context/literature, not local data.

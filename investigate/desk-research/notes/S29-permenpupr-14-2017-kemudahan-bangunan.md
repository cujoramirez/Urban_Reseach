## Scope
**STANDARD (Indonesia, binding — buildings).** The enforceable Indonesian building-accessibility
regulation. With Bina Marga 07/2023 (S13, pedestrian facilities in the road space) it forms the
**binding local baseline**; this one covers the *inside of / approach to* buildings — i.e. the
places the accessibility audit visits.

## QUANTITATIVE standards (exact passages: audit benchmarks)
- **Ramp slope — indoor ≤ 6° (≈ 1:10); outdoor ≤ 5° (≈ 1:12).** (Ramp di dalam bangunan maksimum
  6°/1:10; di luar bangunan maksimum 5°/1:12.)
- **Ramp width ≥ 95 cm** (without safety curb); **≥ 120 cm** (with curb/kanstin).
- **Bordes (landing / rest platform) every ≤ 900 cm** of ramp run.
- **Kanstin (low safety curb) ≥ 10 cm** high — guides low-vision users and keeps wheelchair wheels on
  the ramp.
- **Accessible toilet ("toilet difabel"):** turning space + grab bars + outward-/sliding-door
  (exact dimensions in the Lampiran figures).
- **Guiding block / ubin pemandu (tactile paving)** required for the visually impaired.

## Why it matters
- The **binding** number set for the place audit's interior/approach criteria (ramp, width, landing,
  curb), localized to Indonesia.
- Note the **stricter-than-ADA indoor ramp** (≈1:10 vs ADA 1:12): the baseline scores against the
  **stricter** of the two for safety, and flags steeper ramps as fails.

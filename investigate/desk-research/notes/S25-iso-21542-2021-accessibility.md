# S25: ISO 21542:2021 — Building construction: Accessibility and usability of the built environment

## Full citation
International Organization for Standardization. (2021). *ISO 21542:2021 — Building construction —
Accessibility and usability of the built environment*. Geneva: ISO.

- **Official source page:** https://www.iso.org/standard/71860.html
- **Open scope summary:** ANSI Blog, https://blog.ansi.org/ansi/iso-21542-2021-accessible-building-construction/
- **Source type:** International voluntary standard (ISO).
- **Accessed:** 2026-06-28

## Scope
**STANDARD (international, buildings).** The single international reference for built-environment
accessibility — requirements + recommendations for designing/constructing buildings usable by all,
including wheelchair / cane / scooter users and people with vision and hearing impairment. Paywalled
(CHF), so cited here for scope and framing; concrete dimensions are cross-referenced to ADA 2010
(S26) and Permen PUPR 14/2017 (S29).

## Verified passages
- **Scope (exact):** "specifies a range of requirements and recommendations for the elements of
  construction, building assemblies, components, fittings and products that relate to the design and
  constructional aspects of usability and accessibility of buildings, including access to buildings,
  circulation within buildings, egress from buildings during normal conditions, and evacuation in the
  event of a fire." (ISO 21542:2021 scope, via ISO.org / ANSI)
- Also covers **outdoor features** "directly concerned with access to a building … from a relevant
  site boundary" — i.e. the approach/path, relevant to the audit's *step-free approach* criterion.
- Introduces **"exceptional considerations for existing buildings"** — an explicitly *restricted but
  acceptable* level where full compliance is exceptionally difficult.

## Why it matters
- The authoritative international anchor for the accessibility baseline; lets the place audit and
  Persona 4 cite a real international standard, not opinion (the mentor's exact ask).
- "Exceptional considerations for existing buildings" mirrors the Jakarta reality (retrofitted,
  partly-compliant places) → supports a **graded** verdict (accessible / partial / not), not binary.

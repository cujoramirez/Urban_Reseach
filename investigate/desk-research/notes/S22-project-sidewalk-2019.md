## What it does
Crowdworkers virtually walk Google Street View and label pedestrian-accessibility issues. Five
label types: **curb ramps, missing curb ramps, obstacles, surface problems, no sidewalk.** Pairs
crowdsourcing with **machine learning / CV** to auto-detect and scale labeling.

## Reported scale (from project pages, VERIFY exact)
Deployed across ~35 cities / 8 countries; >1.1 million labels; ~21,380 km of streets analysed.

## Why it matters for us
A ready **label schema** and tool we could apply to Sudirman (check if Jakarta is deployable), and
a model for combining human labels with CV, the same human-in-the-loop our field audit provides as
ground truth.

## Flags
- The 2019 paper reports the early DC deployment; the 35-cities / 1.1M-label figures are current
  project stats, not the paper. Attribute carefully. Confirm Jakarta availability before relying.

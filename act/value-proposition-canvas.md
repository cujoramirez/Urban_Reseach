## VPC 1 (primary, grounded customer): the commuter in a rush, as pedestrian-reporter

### Customer Profile (grounded)

**Customer jobs**

Functional:
- Walk the last mile from the transit node to the office or destination, and back, on time (R03, R05, R06, R08, R10)
- Walk out for lunch midday and back (board)
- Cross the road safely at the zebra crossing or JPO (R05, R10)
- Stay comfortable enough while walking, not overheated or soaked (R02, R03, R05, R07)

Social:
- Move with the flow, not alone, not standing out (R05, R06)
- Arrive presentable, not drenched in sweat (board)

Emotional:
- Feel safe and at ease, especially alone or after dark (R09, N02)
- Feel that a problem I hit could actually be acted on (reporter job; grounded via workers N11, N12; for this persona itself (hyp: Day-2))

**Pains**
- Heat and no shade on exposed stretches; arrive sweaty (R02, R03, R05, R07, R11)
- Rush-hour crush, obstacles, chaos, tidak tertib (N02)
- Kerb-to-zebra ramp too high, steep, slippery; fear of falling; fast vehicles (R05, R10)
- Ojol riding the trotoar, near-misses (R12, N11)
- Broken, uneven surface underfoot (N11, N12)
- Path partly blocked by vendors or people sitting (R12)
- Night isolation at the JPO and parking after 22:00, lit but empty (R09, N11) [time-specific]
- Not knowing the corridor's condition before or during the walk (board)
- When I report a problem, nothing happens and I hear nothing back (N11, N12; for this persona (hyp: Day-2))

**Gains**
- Get there on time and predictably, no nasty surprise (board)
- Stay cool: shade, or knowing the cooler time or shadier route (R02, R03, R04)
- Safe, gentle crossings (R05, R10)
- Order on the path when it is crowded (N02)
- Feel safe when alone or at night (R09, N02)
- Arrive presentable (board)
- Awareness and predictability about what is ahead (board, the whiteboard "satisfied" state)
- Feel heard: see a flagged problem acknowledged and prioritised (N11, N12 inverse; (hyp: Day-2))

### Value Map (solution side, every item a bet to test)

**Products and services** (the report-while-you-walk, sidewalk-first concept)
- A pedestrian-first, sidewalk-level map of Dukuh Atas to Semanggi, not road-derived (hyp; method in COMPUTATIONAL-DIRECTION.md, tile2net)
- In-situ reporting: pin a problem while walking, photo plus location (hyp: Day-2)
- Clustering: co-located reports collapse into one card with a count, tap to see all (hyp)
- Status on each report: acknowledged, in progress, done (hyp; addresses the evidenced JAKI gap)
- A comfort and condition layer: shade and heat exposure, surface condition, lighting and isolation flags (hyp)
- Authority view: a prioritised map of the most-reported, highest-signal spots (hyp; authority side)

**Pain relievers** (each tied to a pain it would kill)
- Shade and timing layer eases the heat pain, know the shaded route or cooler time (hyp, pain: R02, R03, R05, R07, R11)
- Surface, obstruction and ramp flags warn before you hit them (hyp, pain: R05, R10, N11, N12, R12)
- Lighting and isolation flags plus a peopled-route option ease night isolation, framed as environmental factors not crime labels (hyp, pain: R09)
- Report status plus clustering ease the not-heard pain, you see it acknowledged and prioritised (hyp, pain: N11, N12)
- Condition awareness before and during the walk eases unpredictability (hyp, pain: board)

**Gain creators**
- Awareness and predictability: see conditions ahead, the satisfied state from the whiteboard (hyp, gain: board)
- Feeling heard: clustering, status and authority prioritisation make reports visibly matter (hyp, gain: N11, N12)
- Cooler, more comfortable, presentable arrival via shade and timing guidance (hyp, gain: heat findings)
- A safer feeling via environmental awareness, not danger labelling (hyp, gain: R09)
- Contribution and recognition for the super-reporter tier, the good-samaritan role (hyp; analog)

### Fit check (honest)
- Strongest grounded pains: heat, the ramp hazard, ojol on the trotoar, broken surface, unpredictability, and not being heard.
- The relievers map onto those, but every reliever is a bet. The fit is hypothesised, not proven.
- Biggest unproven leaps, in order of how much they would sink the concept:
  1. People will actually report in-situ while walking (hyp: Day-2). If false, the concept stalls.
  2. The JAKI gap is status and visibility (mostly evidenced) rather than duplicates (not evidenced).
  3. Comfort and shade routing is wanted enough to be used, not just nice to hear (hyp: Sat).
- Saturday and Day-2 exist to test these. The concept must survive them, not be shielded from them.

## On the good samaritan (not a separate canvas)
- The super-reporter does not get its own VPC, because we never met one and a canvas needs pains
  and gains that trace to evidence.
- It shows up here in two places: as a **contributor role or tier** inside VPC 1 (the gain creator
  "contribution and recognition"), and as the **reason the authority side matters** (a super-reporter
  who never sees follow-up quits, N11, N12).
- Promote it to its own canvas only if Day-2 or Saturday finds a real one.

## What this VPC is not
- Not a commitment to build. It is a map of bets, with the customer side grounded and the value side
  to be tested.
- Not leaning on the duplicate-backlog premise.
- Not a crime map. Safety appears only as environmental factors (lighting, isolation, sightlines),
  never as criminality or named offenders.

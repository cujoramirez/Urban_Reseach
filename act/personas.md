# User Personas and Journey (Tim Riset Pijak)

> **Stage: Act, but evidence-first.** These personas describe who actually uses the Sudirman
> corridor, built only from Field Day 1 respondents (2026-06-18). They are an input to the
> Value Proposition Canvas, which comes after these are agreed. Nothing here commits us to the
> app; it maps the people and their day.

## How to read this

Every entry is tagged with where it comes from, so we never present a guess as a finding:

- `(R05)`, `(N11)` etc. = grounded in a Day-1 respondent (see
  `../investigate/field/transcripts/2026-06-18-*` and `data/respondents_2026-06-18.csv`).
- `(board)` = carried over from the team's whiteboard / Miro work, consistent with Day-1.
- `(hyp: Day-2)` or `(hyp: Sat)` = a hypothesis we have not tested yet; the field round named
  in the tag is where we check it. Do not treat these as facts.

The 1-10 scores in the source files are the analyst's interpretation of transcripts, not asked
measurements, so they inform direction only.

**Mapping to Miro:** each box below is a stack of one-line bullets. Select a box, copy, paste
into Miro, and accept "create sticky per line" to drop the whole stack onto the board.

## Untested and blindspots (read before the personas)

These holes are why we keep the personas honest. They are Saturday and Day-2 targets, not people
we get to invent now.

- **Back-street gradient.** Almost no back-street sampling (one contact, Pak Lansia Warlok at
  Kebon Melati, N09). Our locked scope is the contrast between the main trotoar and the streets
  one block behind, yet we have near-zero evidence there. `(hyp: Sat)`
- **Women at night.** The night-risk reports come from male security informants describing the
  area (R09, N11), not from women walking it. The one woman who walks home at 01:00 says she
  feels safe (N02). Under-sampled and mixed. `(hyp: Sat)`
- **Reporting while walking.** The pain (complaints ignored) is real (N11, N12). The wish to
  report in the moment, on foot, is not proven. The Day-2 guide is built to test it. `(hyp: Day-2)`
- **Newcomers / first-timers.** Zero Day-1 evidence. We interviewed only regulars, so a
  first-timer persona would be invented.
- **People with disabilities / varied mobility.** Inferred only from the ramp hazard (R05, R10);
  no respondents with disabilities. `(hyp: Sat)`
- **Rain behaviour.** Day 1 was dry (0 mm). Untested. `(hyp: Sat)`
- **The good-samaritan power-reporter.** A real pattern in crowdsourced map apps, but never
  observed on this corridor. Treated as a hypothesis archetype at the end of Part 1, not a
  grounded persona. `(hyp: Day-2)`

# Part 1: Personas

## Persona 1 (primary): The commuter in a rush

**One sentence:** A daily transit commuter who walks the Sudirman last mile under time pressure,
and just wants to reach work or home without the heat, the rush-hour crush, or a nasty surprise
underfoot.

**Demografi (grounded):** Office workers, students and other transit users, roughly 19 to 40,
normal mobility, commuting Monday to Friday from across Jabodetabek (Bogor and Cileungsi by LRT,
Pondok Aren by TJ). The walk is the last leg of a KRL / MRT / TJ / LRT trip, not a choice.
(R03, R05, R06, R08, R10, N02)

**Goals and motivation:** Reach the office, school or home reliably and on time. (R03, R05, R06,
R08, R10) Walking is cheap, healthy, faster than waiting in rush-hour traffic, and the transit
node is close. (board)

**Challenge toward the goal:** The heat midday (R02, R03, R05, R07, R10), the rush-hour crowd and
obstacles (N02), the kerb-to-zebra ramp that is too high, steep and slippery (R05, R10), and ojol
forcing onto the trotoar (R12). The day feels unpredictable. (board)

### Actions, Motivations and Pains

**What do I do?**
- Walk from the station or halte to the office, and back. (R03, R05, R08)
- Walk out to grab lunch around midday. (board)
- Walk the last leg of a transit commute. (R06, R08, R10)

**Why do I do it?**
- It is the commute; the station is close and the walk is short. (R08, board)
- Cheaper than the alternatives. (board)
- Faster than sitting in rush-hour traffic. (board)
- It keeps me a bit healthier. (board)

**What do I want?**
- To get there comfortably and predictably. (board)
- Shade on the exposed stretches. (R03, R05)
- Order and ketertiban when it is crowded. (N02)
- Even paths and a safe, gentle crossing. (R05, R10)

**What is stopping me?**
- Too hot, not enough shade. (R02, R03, R05, R07, R11)
- Rush-hour crush and obstacles on the path. (N02)
- The kerb-to-zebra ramp is too high and slippery; a friend fell. (R05, R10)
- Ojol riding onto the trotoar. (R12)
- Broken and uneven spots underfoot. (N11, N12)
- The trotoar partly blocked by vendors or people sitting. (R12)

### Values

**What convinces me? (external)**
- Origin and destination are close. (board)
- Plenty of transit options. (board)
- Walking alongside other people, not alone. (R05, R06)

**What or who informs me?**
- Google Maps or Apple Maps. (board)
- A weather app. (board)
- Asking people, or social media. (board)
- Exactly which of these, and how, is a Day-2 question. (hyp: Day-2)

### Context

**Where am I?**
- The main Sudirman trotoar, Dukuh Atas to Semanggi. (R03, R05, R08)
- Stations, halte and the JPO. (R05, R08)

**What is my day-to-day?**
- Around 6 AM, transit in, then walk the last leg. (R08, board)
- 12 to 1 PM, walk out for lunch. (board)
- Roughly 5 to 9 PM, transit home. (board, N02)

### Their current solution (and why it falls short)
- Google / Apple Maps: routes me, but knows nothing about shade, heat or path condition. ✗ (board)
- Weather app: tells me the temperature, does not help the walk itself. ✗ (board)
- Asking people / social media: slow and hit or miss. ✗ (board)
- JAKI: a civic complaint channel, not something I check before a walk. ✗ (board)
- Whether this persona reports anything today is unproven. (hyp: Day-2)

### Other personas that interact with them
- Ojol driver: 👍 saves time getting to the office. 🚧 ngetem and rides onto the trotoar. (R12)
- PKL: 👍 affordable food and snacks. 🚧 blocks the pedestrian lane. (R12)
- Other pedestrians: 🚧 crowding, slow walkers, chaos at rush hour. (N02)
- Public-facility worker: 👍 keeps the space maintained and watched. (R01, R12)

## Persona 2: The unhurried regular (well-being walker)

**One sentence:** A regular who walks Sudirman by choice, for health or because they enjoy the
leafy, revitalised corridor, and for whom the heat is the one thing spoiling an otherwise good
walk.

**Demografi (grounded):** Ranges from elderly daily exercisers to younger people walking off-peak
or against the rush. Not time-pressured. They value the environment itself. (R02, R03, R04, R07)

**Goals and motivation:** Health, exercise and enjoyment. They choose to walk because the
corridor is adem (cool and leafy) and feels good. (R04, R02, R10)

**Challenge toward the goal:** Midday heat on the exposed stretches, near Sarinah and the
Japanese Embassy (R04, R03, R02); trees that are not yet effective by day; foliage that covers
the lamps at night; mosquitoes that make sitting in the evening unpleasant. (R11)

### Actions, Motivations and Pains

**What do I do?**
- Walk for exercise or leisure, often off-peak or against the flow. (R04, R03)
- Long loops, for example Thamrin City to Semanggi to HI and back. (R04)

**Why do I do it?**
- I enjoy it; the corridor is adem and shaded. (R04, R02)
- It keeps me healthy. (R04)
- The buffer from the main road keeps it pollution-free. (R10)

**What do I want?**
- Keep the shaded, leafy environment. (R02, R04)
- More canopy or shade on the hot stretches. (R03)
- A walk that stays cool and comfortable. (R02)

**What is stopping me?**
- Midday heat on the exposed stretches. (R04, R03, R02)
- Trees not yet effective by day. (R11)
- Dark under the foliage at night, plus mosquitoes. (R11)

### Values

**What convinces me? (external)**
- Shade and trees. (R02, R04)
- Quiet; it feels safer and nicer when it is not crowded. (R03)
- Police presence around. (R04)

**What or who informs me?**
- My own experience: I pick the cooler hours and the shadier route. (R03, R04)
- How I decide, in detail, is a Day-2 question. (hyp: Day-2)

### Context

**Where am I?**
- The main trotoar, on the shaded stretches. (R04, R02)
- Off-peak or counter-flow times. (R03)

**What is my day-to-day?**
- Daily or several times a week, at times I choose. (R04, R03)
- For example a 1.5 hour morning loop, or a weekly walk when it is quiet. (R04, R03)

### Their current solution (and why it falls short)
- I time the walk and pick the route by my own experience. (R03, R04)
- I cope with the heat by seeking shade and choosing cooler hours. (R02, R04)
- No tool tells me whether a stretch is shaded or pleasant right now. ✗

### Other personas that interact with them
- Other pedestrians: 👍 a bit of company is nice. 🚧 crowding spoils the quiet I prefer. (R03)
- PKL: 👍 some of us like buying a snack along the way. (R12)
- The greenery and trees: 👍 the reason I am here, something I actively value. (R02, R10)

## Persona 3: The corridor worker (ground-level observer)

**One sentence:** Someone who works the corridor all day, sees every broken kerb, ojol near-miss
and night-time risk first-hand, and has stopped expecting anything to happen when they report it.

**Demografi (grounded):** Security guards, transit-operator staff (MITJ), Satpol PP, and vendors.
On the corridor for full shifts, including at night. (R01, R09, R12, N05, N11, N12)

**Goals and motivation:** Keep their patch safe and orderly and do the job. Some genuinely care
about the place and the public. (R01, R12, N12)

**Challenge toward the goal:** Each operator guards only its own zone, so public space is nobody's
job (R01, N05); enforcement falls between agencies (motorbikes are Dishub's remit, not Satpol
PP's) (R12); night isolation at the JPO and parking after 22:00 (R09, N11); broken and uneven
infrastructure (N11, N12); ojol on the trotoar and through the tunnel (R12, N11); and complaints
to government that go nowhere. (N11, N12)

### Actions, Motivations and Pains

**What do I do?**
- Work shifts on the corridor: guard, patrol, enforce, operate, or sell. (R01, R09, R12, N11)
- Mobile plotting across posts: Polda Bawah, Summitmas, Patung Pemuda. (R12)

**Why do I do it?**
- It is the job. (R01, R12)
- Keep the area safe and orderly. (R12)
- Some of us care about the public and the place. (N12)

**What do I want?**
- Real cooperation across operators and agencies, not buck-passing. (R01)
- Motorbike compliance so they stop using the trotoar as a shortcut. (R12)
- Problems actually fixed when we flag them. (N11, N12)

**What is stopping me?**
- Responsibility gaps between zones and agencies. (R01, N05, R12)
- Reports to government ignored, "gak pernah digubris". (N11, N12)
- Ojol violations I have no authority to enforce. (R12)
- Night isolation spots I cannot cover. (R09)

### Values

**What convinces me? (external)**
- Clear ownership and jurisdiction. (R01)
- Visible follow-up when something is reported. (N11, N12)
- What exactly would make them trust a channel again is a Day-2 question. (hyp: Day-2)

**What or who informs me?**
- First-hand daily observation. (R09, R12)
- Word of mouth among workers. (N11)
- Tried the official government channel and got nothing back. (N11, N12)

### Context

**Where am I?**
- My post: the MRT/KAI area, the JPO, the Kendal tunnel, the parking. (R01, R09, R12, N11)
- On shift, including nights. (R09, R12)

**What is my day-to-day?**
- Full shifts, including night. (R09)
- Mobile patrols and plotting; I see the same problems on repeat. (R12)

### Their current solution (and why it falls short)
- I reported the broken, uneven infrastructure to the government. It was ignored. ✗ (N11, N12)
- No feedback, no status, no sense of being heard; it feels like they do not care. ✗ (N12)
- This persona is the clearest evidence of the reporting gap behind the pivot. Whether they
  would use a new reporting tool is still unproven. (hyp: Day-2)

### Other personas that interact with them
- Ojol: 🚧 violate the trotoar and tunnel, near-misses. (R12, N11)
- PKL: 🚧 I have to keep them in order. 👍 but pedestrians like them. (R12)
- Other operators (KAI, MRT, police): 🚧 do not coordinate across zones. (R01, N05)
- The public and pedestrians: 👍 the people I am here to protect.

## Hypothesis persona (archetype, not observed in Day-1): The good-samaritan reporter

> **Read this label first.** We never met this person in the field. This is an archetype borrowed
> from how crowdsourced map products actually work (Waze top contributors and Map Editors, Google
> Local Guides, OpenStreetMap power mappers), plus a teammate's own experience of one friend who
> marked an entire route's hazards, prices and obstructions. It is here because the whole
> report-while-you-walk idea may live or die on a small core of people like this, so we want them
> on the radar. It is NOT evidence. Everything below is `(analog)` from comparable products or
> `(hyp)` to test, except where it connects to a real Day-1 finding.

**One sentence:** The rare person who reports problems for everyone, not just themselves, and keeps
doing it; the 1% who would generate most of the signal the rest of us rely on.

**Why this matters, and the catch:** In crowdsourced platforms a tiny minority creates the
majority of content (the participation-inequality or 90-9-1 pattern). `(analog)` If they do not
show up, a report-while-you-walk app has nothing to show. So this persona is both the most valuable
and the least certain. We have not confirmed a single one exists on this corridor.

**Nearest real candidate we did meet:** Persona 3, the corridor worker. They already see everything
all day and already tried to report (N11, N12). The good-samaritan is roughly the activated,
app-enabled version of that latent observer. That bridge is an inference, not a finding.

### Actions, Motivations and Pains

**What do I do?**
- Report hazards, obstructions and conditions for the benefit of strangers (analog; teammate anecdote)
- Mark a whole route, not just my own problem (teammate anecdote) `(hyp)`

**Why do I do it?**
- I want to help other people and the place `(hyp)`
- Recognition and a sense of contribution (analog) `(hyp)`
- Civic pride `(hyp)`

**What do I want?**
- My reports to actually be seen and acted on (connects to N11, N12)
- To know I made a difference `(hyp)`

**What is stopping me?**
- If reports go nowhere, I burn out fast; no follow-up kills contribution (grounded risk: N11, N12)
- No recognition or feedback loop (analog) `(hyp)`

### Values

**What convinces me? (external)**
- Visible impact, reports that get acted on (analog) `(hyp)`
- Community or status recognition (analog) `(hyp)`

**What or who informs me?**
- My own constant observation; I am the source others rely on `(hyp)`

### Context

**Where am I?**
- All over the corridor, often, with high exposure `(hyp)`

**What is my day-to-day?**
- Frequently present and habitually reporting `(hyp)`

### Relationship to the other personas
- I generate the signal that the commuter, and any future newcomer, would consume. The app's
  two-sided value depends on me existing. 👍 `(hyp)`

### How we test whether this persona is real (do not skip)
- Day-2 probe: "Pernah nggak kamu lapor atau nandain sesuatu buat kepentingan orang lain, bukan
  cuma masalah kamu sendiri? Mau nggak jadi orang kayak gitu? Apa yang bikin kamu mau terus
  ngelakuinnya?" `(hyp: Day-2)`
- Watch for people who already do this informally: RT/RW or community WhatsApp groups, local-issue
  X/Twitter accounts, neighbourhood Facebook. `(hyp: Day-2)`
- The authority side of the VPC matters most for this persona: without visible follow-up, they quit.

# Part 2: User Journey (primary persona, the commuter in a rush)

The team's board maps the office worker's day. This is that journey, grounded where we can and
flagged where we cannot. The feeling at each phase is placed on the team's BAD to GOOD strip
(anxiety, resah, panik, lelah, frustasi, kepanasan, tidak didengar, uncertainty on the bad side;
tenang, puas on the good side). The day currently sits mostly on the bad side.

| Time | Before | Trigger | During | After | Feeling |
|------|--------|---------|--------|-------|---------|
| **Pagi** (commute in) | Get off the transit and start the last leg on the corridor (R08, board) | Rush hour (board, N02) | Crush and obstacles on the path, "banyak rintangan" (N02, board) | Made it, but worn out (board) | lelah, resah |
| **Siang** (lunch walk) | Leave the office to grab lunch (board) | Harsh midday sun, vehicle fumes (board, R02, R10) | Walk and queue for food in the heat (board) | Sweat-soaked shirt, "bau matahari", malu and kesal (board) | kepanasan, frustasi |
| **Sore** (commute home) | Leave the office to go home (board) | Rush hour again (board) | Crush and chaos in the transit (board) | Drained, the energy went into the journey (board) | lelah, drained |
| **Malem** (late home) `(hyp: Sat)` | Leave the office late (board) | Walking alone, the trotoar quiet, broken spots hard to see (board, R09, N11) | Anxious and uneasy walking alone (board scenario; our N02 woman felt safe and R09 says risk is real but falling, so this row is the least tested) | Risk of tripping on an unseen broken kerb (board, N11, N12) | anxiety, uncertainty |

**Where the other personas diverge (so we do not over-generalise):**
- **The unhurried walker** has no rush-hour crush; their journey is a chosen walk where the only
  real low is the midday heat on the exposed stretches. The Pagi and Sore crush rows do not apply.
  (R02, R03, R04)
- **The corridor worker** is present across all four phases as a fixed observer, not a traveller.
  Their low is not the commute but the "tidak didengar" feeling after a report goes nowhere, and
  the night isolation they cannot cover. (R09, N11, N12)

**The night row is our weakest link.** It is mostly board narrative plus second-hand security
accounts. Saturday's women-led night intercepts are what turn it from a guess into evidence.

====================================================================
VPC 1 (PRIMARY, GROUNDED): THE COMMUTER IN A RUSH, AS PEDESTRIAN-REPORTER
CUSTOMER PROFILE (left side, grounded)
====================================================================

[Customer jobs: functional]
Walk the last mile from the transit node to work and back, on time (R03, R05, R06, R08, R10)
Walk out for lunch midday and back (board)
Cross the road safely at the zebra crossing or JPO (R05, R10)
Stay comfortable while walking, not overheated or soaked (R02, R03, R05, R07)

[Customer jobs: social]
Move with the flow, not alone, not standing out (R05, R06)
Arrive presentable, not drenched in sweat (board)

[Customer jobs: emotional]
Feel safe and at ease, especially alone or after dark (R09, N02)
Feel that a problem I hit could actually be acted on (via workers N11, N12; for this persona hyp: Day-2)

[Pains]
Heat and no shade on exposed stretches, arrive sweaty (R02, R03, R05, R07, R11)
Rush-hour crush, obstacles, chaos, tidak tertib (N02)
Kerb-to-zebra ramp too high, steep, slippery, fear of falling (R05, R10)
Ojol riding the trotoar, near-misses (R12, N11)
Broken, uneven surface underfoot (N11, N12)
Path partly blocked by vendors or people sitting (R12)
Night isolation at the JPO and parking after 22:00, lit but empty (R09, N11)
Not knowing the corridor condition before or during the walk (board)
I report a problem and nothing happens, I hear nothing back (N11, N12; this persona hyp: Day-2)

[Gains]
Get there on time and predictably, no nasty surprise (board)
Stay cool: shade, or knowing the cooler time or shadier route (R02, R03, R04)
Safe, gentle crossings (R05, R10)
Order on the path when it is crowded (N02)
Feel safe when alone or at night (R09, N02)
Arrive presentable (board)
Awareness and predictability about what is ahead (board)
Feel heard: a flagged problem acknowledged and prioritised (N11, N12 inverse; hyp: Day-2)


====================================================================
VPC 1: VALUE MAP (right side, every sticky is a bet to test)
====================================================================

[Products and services]
A pedestrian-first, sidewalk-level map of Dukuh Atas to Semanggi, not road-derived (hyp; tile2net)
In-situ reporting: pin a problem while walking, photo plus location (hyp: Day-2)
Clustering: co-located reports collapse into one card with a count, tap to see all (hyp)
Status on each report: acknowledged, in progress, done (hyp; the evidenced JAKI gap)
Comfort and condition layer: shade and heat, surface condition, lighting and isolation flags (hyp)
Authority view: a prioritised map of the most-reported spots (hyp)

[Pain relievers]
Shade and timing layer eases the heat pain (hyp, pain: R02, R03, R05, R07, R11)
Surface, obstruction and ramp flags warn before you hit them (hyp, pain: R05, R10, N11, N12, R12)
Lighting and isolation flags plus peopled-route option ease night isolation, environmental not crime (hyp, pain: R09)
Report status plus clustering ease the not-heard pain (hyp, pain: N11, N12)
Condition awareness before and during the walk eases unpredictability (hyp, pain: board)

[Gain creators]
Awareness and predictability, see conditions ahead (hyp, gain: board)
Feeling heard, clustering plus status plus authority prioritisation (hyp, gain: N11, N12)
Cooler, presentable arrival via shade and timing guidance (hyp, gain: heat findings)
A safer feeling via environmental awareness, not danger labelling (hyp, gain: R09)
Contribution and recognition for the super-reporter tier (hyp; analog)

[Fit check, biggest unproven leaps]
People will actually report in-situ while walking, if false the concept stalls (hyp: Day-2)
The JAKI gap is status and visibility, not duplicates (mostly evidenced vs not evidenced)
Comfort and shade routing is wanted enough to be used, not just nice to hear (hyp: Sat)

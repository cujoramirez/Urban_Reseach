Reading the colours: the Customer Profile (right) comes out mostly solid, we know the customer. The Value Map (left) is almost all dashed, the solution is still a set of bets. That gap is the work Saturday and Day-2 have to close.
  • Three leaps that would sink the concept, in order: (1) people actually report in-situ while walking (Day-2); (2) the JAKI gap is status and visibility, not duplicates; (3) comfort and shade routing is wanted enough to use (Sat).
  • Good samaritan: a contributor tier inside VPC 1, not its own canvas, and the reason the authority side matters.
  • Not a commitment to build. Not leaning on the duplicate-backlog premise. Not a crime map: safety is environmental factors only, never criminality or named offenders.
